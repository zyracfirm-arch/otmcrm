Founded by Chef Ubedulla Mansuri in the heart of Khamasa, Ahmedabad, Over The Moon (OTM) was born with a single mission: to elevate vegetarian street food into an unforgettable gourmet experience. Every patty is handcrafted from scratch, every cheese burst is molten to perfection, and every dip is blended in-house daily.
